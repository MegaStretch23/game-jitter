Game Jitter

Paste ping results or load a ping or mtr -r output file. You get mean, p95 and p99 latency, jitter, packet loss, spike count and a plain verdict for competitive play: good, borderline or bad.

Runs in this tab. Your samples are not uploaded or stored: the page makes no network requests of its own and has no analytics.

      How it is computed

      • Jitter: mean absolute difference between consecutive replies (a lost packet breaks the pair).
      • p95 / p99: nearest-rank percentiles of the replies. Below 20 replies they are not meaningful, and the page says so.
      • Spike: a reply above the larger of 2 x median and median + 30 ms.
      • Verdict: bad if packet loss ≥ 2%, jitter ≥ 30 ms, p99 ≥ 150 ms or spikes ≥ 5% of replies; good if loss ≤ 0.5%, jitter ≤ 10 ms, p99 ≤ 100 ms and spikes ≤ 2%; otherwise borderline. These cutoffs are this tool's own rule of thumb, not a standard.
      • mtr report files carry one summary line per hop, so only loss, mean, fastest, slowest and standard deviation are available.
